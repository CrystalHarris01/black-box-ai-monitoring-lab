Security Analysis

The packet captures demonstrate how AI monitoring controls can be validated at the network layer. Because the lab intentionally uses plaintext HTTP over localhost, the prompts and policy responses are visible inside packet inspection.

  • AI prompts may expose sensitive data if transmitted without encryption.
  • Packet captures can validate monitoring boundaries and application-layer controls.
  • Wireshark evidence can support governance and assurance documentation.
  • Production AI systems should use TLS and avoid logging sensitive prompt content.
